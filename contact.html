Booking policies

Quotes and bookings

Package rates are indicative. The written quote sets out the services, inclusions and pricing for your event. Confirm availability and scope with Ojasva before booking.

Payments

Your written booking terms confirm the advance amount, payment schedule and approved payment method.

Changes, cancellations and refunds

Review the cancellation, rescheduling and refund terms in your booking agreement before paying an advance. Contact Ojasva as soon as your date or requirements change.

How this enquiry form works

Fill in your event details and submit the form. Your inquiry is securely recorded and our team will get in touch with you shortly. You can also download a copy of your brief.
